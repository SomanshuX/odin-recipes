Hot Chocolate with Dark Chocolate

Ingredients:

  1. Milk
  2. Dark Chocolate
  3. Cocoa Powder (optional)
  4. Sugar

Procedure:

  1. Take the milk in a saucepan and put it on high flame.
  2. Add some grated dark chocolate
  3. Stir for a minute or two.
  4. Now add some cocoa powder.
  5. Add some sugar depending on taste.
  6. Wait it the milk is almost about to boil; keep stirring.
  7. Turn off the flame. Your hot chocolate is ready to be served!
